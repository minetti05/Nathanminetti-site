    summary: "Explore how your brand or business can participate more meaningfully in culture."
    format: "Half-day or full-day"
    draft: true
    problem: "Your brand shows up in culture through campaigns, but not in a way your audience would miss if it stopped."
    attend: "Brand, marketing, creative and communications leads, plus the agency partners who work with them."
    session: "We look at your audience and the cultural territories they care about, then find credible ways to take part through partnerships, content and experiences."
    outputs:
      - "Cultural and audience opportunity assessment"
      - "Relevant cultural territories"
      - "Partnership and collaboration opportunities"
      - "Creative and experiential concepts"

  - title: "The Global-to-Local Opportunity Sprint"
    summary: "Turn global ambitions into actionable local market opportunities."
    format: "Half-day or full-day"
    draft: true
    problem: "A global strategy arrives in market without the local insight, partners or priorities it needs to work."
    attend: "Global and regional brand leads, local market teams and the agencies that support them."
    session: "We translate the global ambition into local priorities, audiences and partners, and build a practical 90-day plan."
    outputs:
      - "Local market opportunity assessment"
      - "Priority audiences and stakeholders"
      - "Partnership and growth opportunities"
      - "Market-specific activation priorities"
      - "Practical 90-day action plan"
