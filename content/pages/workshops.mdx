# The first workshop is the flagship, shown first and largest.
workshops:
  - title: "The Partnership Opportunity Lab"
    lines:
      - "Stop asking who might sponsor us. Start asking what we could build together."
      - "Stop buying logos and placements. Start building partnerships that pay off for both sides."
    problem: "Partnership conversations default to sponsorship: a rate card on one side and a logo on the other. The more valuable partnership, where both sides bring something the other can't, rarely gets explored."
    attend: "The people who own partnerships, marketing and commercial strategy, with a senior decision-maker in the room. This works for brands buying partnerships and for rights-holders selling them."
    method: "We start from what you uniquely have: audiences, assets, IP, distribution, relationships. We map the partners who need it and what each side could build. Then we develop the strongest ideas into partnership concepts and rank them by value and feasibility."
    deliverables:
      - "Partnership opportunity assessment"
      - "Potential partner profiles and target list"
      - "Commercial opportunity map"
      - "Initial partnership concepts"
      - "Recommended next steps"
    outcome: "A prioritized partnership pipeline with the commercial case for each, ready to take to market."
  - title: "The Culture Connection Sprint"
    lines:
      - "Move beyond cultural relevance as a marketing talking point."
    problem: "Most brands talk about cultural relevance in campaign briefs. Few have a clear view of which cultural territories they have a real right to be in, or how to take part in a way people would value."
    attend: "Brand, marketing, creative and communications leads, plus agency partners."
    method: "We map your audiences and the cultural territories they care about, test where your brand has credibility, and connect those territories to partners, creators and experiences."
    deliverables:
      - "Cultural and audience opportunity assessment"
      - "Relevant cultural territories"
      - "Partnership and collaboration opportunities"
      - "Creative and experiential concepts"
      - "Strategic recommendations"
    outcome: "A short list of cultural opportunities your brand can credibly own, each with the partners and concepts to activate it."
  - title: "The Global-to-Local Opportunity Sprint"
    lines:
      - "Turn global ambition into local commercial action."
    problem: "Global strategies reach local markets as guidelines, without the local audiences, partners or priorities they need to drive results."
    attend: "Global and regional brand leads, local market teams, and the agencies that support them."
    method: "We start from the global ambition, identify the local audiences and stakeholders who matter most, and connect them to the partnerships and activations that turn the strategy into local growth."
    deliverables:
      - "Local market opportunity assessment"
      - "Priority audiences and stakeholders"
      - "Partnership and growth opportunities"
      - "Market-specific activation priorities"
      - "Practical 90-day action plan"
    outcome: "A local plan with clear priorities and a 90-day path to action, aligned with the global strategy."
