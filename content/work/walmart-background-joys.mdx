## Challenge
Holiday shoppers spend hours with ambient "room" videos playing in the background, but brands had no way to make that viewing shoppable.

## Opportunity
Turn a lean-back format into a lean-in shopping moment, with every gift and piece of decor on screen available at Walmart.

## Approach
Google search data shaped four rooms around different holiday needs: a wintery chalet, a holiday party, a ride with Santa's reindeer and Luke's Diner from Stars Hollow, with ASMR-style sound, holiday music and Easter eggs. Distribution covered Hulu, Roku, MAX, TikTok and Meta, with full videos on YouTube.

I partnered with legal on contract negotiations and partnership reviews with Warner Bros.

I was Account Lead and Executive Producer, and led media strategy and partnerships for the program.
