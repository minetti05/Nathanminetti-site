## Challenge
In 2019, young travelers saw trains as slower and older than flying or driving.

## Opportunity
Stop competing on speed and sell the experience, the part of the trip only a train gives you.

## Approach
"Get Carried Away" celebrated the journey itself. Video, influencer content and native partnerships with travel creators, across digital and social with geotargeting.

I led digital creative, media partnerships, loyalty and innovation for the campaign.

## Results
Purchase intent up 11.4% among young travelers; over 300,000 new riders in four months; Effie finalist. Results as reported in the [Effie case study](https://effie.org/case_studies/case/US_2020_E-4577-117).
