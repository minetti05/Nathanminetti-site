## Challenge
Make a holiday retail message feel like a gift to fans rather than an interruption.

## Opportunity
Earn a place inside a beloved entertainment world instead of borrowing it, which had never been granted to a brand before.

## Approach
The show's key animators created the spot, with Tom Kenny providing the official voice-over, and it was filled with nostalgic references to the show.

I partnered with legal on contract negotiations and partnership reviews with Nickelodeon.
