## Challenge
Give a fusion product a global brand idea that could carry many campaigns, not just one launch.

## Opportunity
Make the product truth, tea fused with fruit and herbs, into a cultural point of view.

## Approach
A platform of connected campaigns, launched across European markets on TV, online video, social, OOH and influencers.

Launched across 87 markets globally, with an initial awareness push across European markets.
