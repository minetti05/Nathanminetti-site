## Challenge
Give a fusion product a global brand idea that could carry many campaigns, not just one launch.

## Opportunity
Make the product truth, tea fused with fruit and herbs, into a cultural point of view.

## Approach
A platform of connected campaigns on TV, online video, social, OOH and influencers. Launched across 87 markets globally, with an initial awareness push across European markets.

As Global Account & Growth Lead, I worked on the pitch and development in 2021, ahead of the 2022 launch.
