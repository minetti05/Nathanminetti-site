## Challenge
Mixed-breed shelter animals get overlooked next to purebreds.

## Opportunity
New Yorkers love their neighborhoods, so give every mix a pedigree from the city itself.

## Approach
An integrated campaign that presented shelter animals as New Yorkers with diverse heritage.

I led account, strategy, innovation and partnerships, and produced the campaign.

## Results
93.2% placement rate (99% during the campaign); adoptions up 7% year over year; every kitten adopted during breeding season for the first time; Adweek Media Plan of the Year, Best Cause Marketing.

Boroughbreds became ACC's lasting brand language, still used years later.
