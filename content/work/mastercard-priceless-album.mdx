## Challenge
Take a sonic brand identity beyond a jingle and make it something people choose to listen to.

## Opportunity
Give artists the brand melody as raw material and let it carry into real music culture.

## Approach
Ten songs by ten artists, from Algeria to Australia and Slovenia to Sweden, unveiled at the Cannes Lions Festival and released on Spotify and other streaming platforms.

[Draft for approval: The album was made with executive producer Niclas Molinder, in partnership with Timbaland's Beatclub, and released first on Spotify.]

I was Partnerships Lead on the album. I partnered with legal on contract negotiations and partnership reviews with the album's 10 artists.
